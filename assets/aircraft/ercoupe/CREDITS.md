# ERCO Ercoupe model

- **Source:** FlightGear FGAddon, Aircraft/ercoupe, downloaded from the official FlightGear aircraft mirror (mirrors.ibiblio.org/flightgear/ftp/Aircraft-2020)
- **Authors:** FlightGear Ercoupe contributors
- **License:** GPL-2.0-or-later (README: "released under the GPL") (see `LICENSE` in this folder)

## Files used

Models/ercoupe.ac, Models/Yoke.ac and the textures they reference.

## Changes

None to the files themselves. Machina reads the files at runtime, hides optional, interior or alternate objects, places sub-models with FlightGear's own offsets, and groups the named objects into selectable parts.
