# Boeing 737-300 model

- **Source:** FlightGear FGAddon, Aircraft/737-300, downloaded from the official FlightGear aircraft mirror (mirrors.ibiblio.org/flightgear/ftp/Aircraft-2020)
- **Authors:** FlightGear 737-300 contributors
- **License:** GPL-3.0 (see `LICENSE` in this folder)

## Files used

Models/737-300.ac and the textures it references (white default livery).

## Changes

`733_machina.png` is a modified copy of `733_white.png`: Machina painted a navy lower fuselage, an amber cheatline and a navy tail with an amber band over the white template, keeping its panel lines. It is distributed under the same GPL-3.0 license. The other files are unchanged. Machina reads them at runtime, hides interior and optional objects, places sub-models with FlightGear's own offsets, and groups the named objects into selectable parts.

These files are distributed under their own license, separately from Machina's code, which is MIT licensed.
